Privacy & community rules

Short and plain, so you actually read it.

What we collect

  • If you just browse: nothing that identifies you. Your watchlist, your own Masala Meter ratings, the apps you ticked and light/dark mode are saved only in your browser, on your device. We never see them.
  • Visit counts: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without building a profile of you.
  • If you post an audience review: we store the display name you chose, your ratings, your review text and the time. We also keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your IP address, only to stop spam and repeat posting. We don't ask for your email or phone number.
  • Spam check: the review box uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check you're a person. It's run by Cloudflare under their privacy policy.

Children

Audience reviews are for adults: you need to confirm you're 18 or over to post. Family Picks never asks for or stores anything about children.

Where it's stored, and for how long

Reviews are stored on Cloudflare's servers. We read each one before it goes up; once approved it's shown publicly on the site under your display name until it's removed. Film details and posters come from TMDB; streaming info comes from TMDB and JustWatch.

Removing a review

Use the Report button on any review, including your own, and tell us if you want it taken down. Reviews with several reports are hidden automatically until we check them.

Community rules for reviews

  • Talk about the film or series, not about people. No abuse, hate, threats or harassment, including about actors, communities, religions or languages.
  • No spoilers without a warning at the start (for example, "Spoiler:").
  • No links, ads, self-promotion or fake reviews.
  • Nothing illegal, sexual or violent, and no one's personal details.
  • Write it yourself. Don't copy reviews from elsewhere.

We may hide or delete reviews that break these rules, without notice.
