📲 Today's WhatsApp post

A ready post for your channel, in the short house layout: pick a new arrival on OTT or one of our reviews, change the words if you like, then copy it or open WhatsApp. The link shows the poster by itself. For a title we've reviewed it uses our Masala Meter, tagline and verdict.

Posting to the channel: WhatsApp doesn't let a link write a channel update for you, so the post is copied first. When the channel opens, tap the message box, press and hold, choose Paste, then send. "Send to a chat or group" opens your phone's share list (or WhatsApp's own chat picker) with the post already filled in.

💾 Backup

Downloads one file with readers' reviews, wrong-info reports, feedback and your corrections. Do it once a week and keep the file somewhere private (it isn't stored anywhere else). Alert sign-ups and visitor accounts are left out on purpose.

💬 Post our take on any film

While you're signed in on this device, open any film or review page and scroll to Audience reviews. The form there says "Post our take": it goes up at once as Masala Mischief team, pinned on top, with no spam check. You can also hide or delete any review on the page itself, and use the 🛡️ button at the bottom left for shortcuts.
