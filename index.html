The idea

Why should masala be serious?

Masala Mischief is built around a simple thought: food should make you smile before the first bite is even over.

We love Indian flavours in all their glorious forms — earthy, tangy, smoky, spicy and wonderfully unpredictable. Our job is to bring that spirit to the table with a fresh, playful point of view.

Come hungry. Leave grinning.
